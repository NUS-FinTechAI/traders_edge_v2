# Learning-product reference study

Reviewed 4 October 2026. The owner prefers Fieldbook's structure, rejects its parchment/forest/serif treatment, and explicitly likes Brilliant and Duolingo's character-led look and feel. The chosen mascot subject is a small, thoughtful, expressive fox. This refines the direction; it does not approve the finished visual system or product implementation.

These are observations of public/promotional surfaces, not logged-in product tests. Exact competitor fonts, colour values, accessibility, performance and learning outcomes were not verified. No competitor artwork or interface code is copied into the repository. A familiar palette alone neither proves nor disproves good design; the useful evidence is hierarchy, consistency and the role of the artwork.

## Refined candidate

Keep Fieldbook's current lesson, prerequisite route and explanation/retry flow. Replace the vintage stationery treatment with white/cool-neutral reading surfaces, charcoal text, a clear blue action colour and restrained warmth from the fox. Use a deliberate sans hierarchy and comfortable rounded controls. This palette is a design proposal based on the owner's preference, not a claim that competitors establish a correct colour scheme.

Remove the ornamental compass, the star next to an unearned task and repeated orientation labels. Use stars only where their learning meaning is explicit. Replace notebook-themed filler with literal task copy. The dashboard gets a compact route summary; the map screen retains connected geography, its optional branch and a destination representing learned explanations.

The fox accompanies short guidance. It does not act as a financial authority, imply live chat, react to virtual profit, express disappointment after a missed day or encourage more trades. Incorrect answers receive a neutral explanation and another attempt. The first asset is a static candidate; a complete expression/animation system is not implemented or approved.

## Verification before presentation

Review the actual dashboard, map and quiz at 390px, all six diagnostic states at 360/390/430/768px, doubled text, touch targets, contrast, reduced motion and quiz recovery. Compare against the previous Fieldbook captures. Count local asset bytes: a mascot can add personality while still adding download cost. Preserve its aspect ratio to avoid layout shift. Static checks do not establish production speed or real beginner comprehension.

The owner reviews the refined screens before final tokens, component specifications or implementation. The earlier 81/90 Fieldbook score belongs to the first exploration and is not a score for this revision.
