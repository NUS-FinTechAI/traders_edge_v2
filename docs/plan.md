## Required review evidence

- Correctness: exact source/contract, successful path, rejected path, retry and reload. Existing tests that miss the requested behavior are not sufficient.
- Safety: no profit, volume or order-count rewards; every simulated order has a prior written plan; reasoned abstention remains valid; no urgency or loss-chasing copy.
- Design: approved direction and meaningful map; actual 390px before/after evidence; 360/430/768px checks; 44px targets, focus, contrast, non-color state and reduced motion.
- Learning: entry diagnosis is distinct from exit assessment; documented remediation; optional bonus rules; reflection presence is not assessed reflection quality; repeated questions are not an unseen transfer test.
- Delivery: narrow scope, updated documentation, passing relevant local checks and hosted CI before merge. Do not label a gated phase complete while approval or evidence is missing.
