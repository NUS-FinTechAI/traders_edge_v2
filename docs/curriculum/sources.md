# Source register

Primary investor-education sources were read on 2026-10-04. A reading date records source inspection, not independent approval of the authored lesson. Research papers below support specific mathematical definitions only. Examples, question wording, arithmetic inputs, progression rules and review intervals are authored for this project.

The core omits local taxes, named account benefits, deposit-protection limits, settlement-day counts and margin thresholds. Source pages can change; review the exact content edition before publication.

## Lesson traceability

These references support the financial concepts. The nine-step teaching sequence and hypothetical decisions implement the source brief; they are not presented as tested interventions. Outcome-versus-process discussion follows that brief’s stated assessment design. Every question inherits the source basis of its lesson; module checks combine their module’s lessons.

| Lesson                                             | Objective                                                                                        | Source IDs                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `m01-l01` · Give money a job                       | Separate essential near-term spending from money that can face uncertain outcomes.               | `sec-start`, `sec-horizon`                                  |
| `m01-l02` · Willing is different from able         | Distinguish comfort with uncertainty from the capacity to absorb a loss.                         | `sec-horizon`, `finra-risk`                                 |
| `m01-l03` · Readiness can mean waiting             | Explain why preserving flexibility can be a reason to avoid an unsuitable opportunity.           | `sec-start`, `finra-risk`                                   |
| `m02-l01` · Follow the roles                       | Distinguish an asset, a broker and a trading venue.                                              | `finra-stocks`, `sec-execution`                             |
| `m02-l02` · Read both sides of a quote             | Calculate a quoted spread and recognize that quotes are time- and quantity-specific.             | `sec-spread`, `sec-execution`                               |
| `m02-l03` · Liquidity is not a promise             | Identify the risk of being unable to transact promptly at an acceptable price.                   | `finra-risk`, `sec-execution`                               |
| `m03-l01` · Ownership has uncertain returns        | Distinguish share-price changes and dividends from guaranteed income.                            | `finra-stocks`                                              |
