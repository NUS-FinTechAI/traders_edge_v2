# 0001: Learning authority and safety

Status: accepted for discovery and design. Product implementation remains gated.

## Context

The supplied research brief requires readiness, risk and investing foundations before execution and optional speculative trading. The screen mockup and v1 put strategies and technical tools earlier. The mockup also uses a “Sniper” badge, “Keep trading” copy and a reward countdown. These conflict with the brief’s focus on decision quality and its avoid list.

## Options

Copy the old sequence and visuals; retain the screen mechanics while applying the brief; or remove gamification entirely.

## Decision

Use the research brief as the product authority. Retain the screen inventory, treasure-map level navigation, normal and bonus stars, learning streaks, archive, profile rewards and process-based ranks. Design their visual appearance independently.

Use the ten-module sequence in the brief. Place analytical reasoning in Making Decisions and technical or leveraged trading pathways behind prerequisites to be specified and reviewed in Phase 2. Do not move a v1 lesson into production merely because it exists.

Drop the “Sniper” badge and “Keep trading” tagline. Use learning language such as “Continue learning.” Remove reward countdowns rather than replacing them with a different urgency cue. A learning streak may record a daily check-in, but its display must not imply that an order is needed to preserve it.

Leaderboards may compare learning XP or multiplayer process-based rank points. Do not feed profit, excess return, final account value, trade count or volume into ranking or reward formulas. Require written plans before simulated orders, and gate multiplayer and endless practice on mastery.

## Consequences

The old screen structure remains recognizable while old scoring and unlock rules must be rewritten. A learner choosing not to trade can still demonstrate mastery. Visual design and numerical mastery criteria still require the Phase 2 process; this decision does not approve them.
