# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions and any event or classroom session run with
the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat other participants respectfully, whatever their experience,
  background or identity.
- Critique the work, not the person. Give feedback that someone can act on.
- Assume good intent, ask before concluding, and allow time for replies.
- Explain disagreements with a reproduction, a source or a tradeoff.
- Respect the privacy of learners, study participants and contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or sexualised language and
  imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information, including participant
  records, account identifiers and contact details, without their permission.
- Encouraging others to break this code.

## Reporting

To raise a concern privately, open an issue asking a maintainer for a private
contact, without including any details of the concern. A maintainer will
reply with a private channel. If the concern involves a maintainer, ask for a
contact with a different maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially as
far as the circumstances allow.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports and decide on a response proportionate to the
behaviour. Responses range from a private note, to removing or editing
contributions, to a temporary or permanent restriction from participating.
Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same
responses.
